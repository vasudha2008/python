Role Model - Jawaharlal Nehru

Jawaharlal Nehru was the first Prime Minister of independent India. He played a key role in India's freedom struggle and worked tirelessly to build a strong, democratic, and modern nation. His leadership and vision helped shape the future of the country.

Nehru believed that education, science, and technology were the foundations of national development. He encouraged young people to work hard, think creatively, and contribute to the progress of India. He also established many educational and scientific institutions.

Jawaharlal Nehru loved children and was affectionately known as "Chacha Nehru." His birthday, 14th November, is celebrated as Children's Day in India. His dedication to the nation, love for children, and commitment to development make him a great role model.
